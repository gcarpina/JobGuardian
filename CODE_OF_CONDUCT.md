# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, professional, and respectful environment for everyone participating in the JobGuardian community.

Contributors, maintainers, users, and community members are expected to collaborate constructively and respectfully regardless of:

- experience level
- technical background
- nationality
- ethnicity
- religion
- disability
- gender identity
- sexual orientation
- personal characteristics

Our goal is to build a healthy technical community focused on learning, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

---

# Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and professional
- providing constructive feedback
- accepting feedback gracefully
- focusing discussions on technical topics and project goals
- helping other contributors learn and grow
- assuming good intentions
- communicating with empathy and patience

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment or discrimination of any kind
- personal attacks or insults
- trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior
- intimidation or threats
- offensive or derogatory comments
- publishing private information without permission
- repeated unconstructive arguments
- any conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment

---

# Technical Disagreements

Technical discussions and disagreements are expected in an engineering-focused project.

When disagreements occur:

- focus on facts and technical reasoning
- explain trade-offs and alternatives
- avoid personal criticism
- remain respectful even when strongly disagreeing
- prioritize the long-term success of the project

Architectural and technical decisions should be challenged through evidence, experimentation, and constructive discussion.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues
- pull requests
- discussions
- documentation reviews
- community communication channels
- any other project-related interactions

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

- issuing warnings
- editing or removing inappropriate content
- temporarily restricting participation
- permanently banning individuals from project spaces

Enforcement decisions will be made with the goal of protecting the community and maintaining a productive environment.

---

# Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers.

When reporting an issue, please provide:

- a description of the incident
- links or references when available
- any relevant context

Reports will be handled confidentially whenever possible.

---

# Project Philosophy

JobGuardian aims to foster a collaborative engineering culture based on:

- respect
- professionalism
- technical excellence
- knowledge sharing
- long-term maintainability

We value constructive contributions more than individual opinions and prioritize the success of the community over individual preferences.
